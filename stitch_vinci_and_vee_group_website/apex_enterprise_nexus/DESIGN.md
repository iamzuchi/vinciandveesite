## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the vision, scale, and multi-industry execution of an enterprise technology and infrastructure conglomerate. Rooted in the foundational principle of "Achieving Dreams by making Connection," the visual personality is authoritative, visionary, structurally precise, and forward-looking. 

The design combines **Modern Corporate** structure with **High-Contrast Precision**:
- **Architectural Rigor:** Clean grid lines, solid proportional blocks, and confident spatial density reflect physical infrastructure, telecommunications, and heavy logistics.
- **Dynamic Technological Edge:** High-contrast vibrant accents against structured dark and light canvases evoke live optical fibre transit, real-time cloud data streams, and active global fleet tracking.
- **Enterprise Dignity:** Restrained ornament, premium finishes, and measured visual pacing evoke established trust for enterprise clients, government infrastructure partners, institutional investors, and wholesale buyers.

## Colors

The palette organizes multi-vertical enterprise operations through a disciplined hierarchy:

- **Primary Canvas & Authority (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Deep naval slate provides corporate grounding, high-contrast typography, and foundational navigation bars. It commands institutional strength across executive dashboards and investor portals.
- **Connectivity & Digital Core (`#0284C7`, `#0EA5E9`):** Vibrant electric cyan represents optical fibre deployment, SaaS telemetries, cloud networks, and real-time connectivity states. It commands interactive focus, primary actions, and live statuses.
- **Dynamic Commerce & Transit (`#F59E0B`):** Energized amber-gold highlights physical operations, supply chain dispatch, tyre distribution metrics, Swedish marine shipping corridors, and heavy equipment infrastructure initiatives.
- **Neutral Surface & Slate (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#64748B`):** Pure white cards set on crisp, cool slate canvas grounds allow complex operational data tables and high-density metrics to remain exceptionally legible.

### Application Rules
1. Never mix cyan and amber as competing visual calls-to-action on the same level. Use cyan for primary system actions and data streams; reserve amber for operational alerts, physical commerce milestones, and transit indicators.
2. Dark backgrounds (`#0F172A`) are reserved for master navigation framing, high-level executive summaries, and dark-mode data visualizations; content canvases use crisp, light slate (`#F8FAFC`) with pure white card surfaces.

## Typography

The pairing combines the architectural, progressive geometry of **Space Grotesk** for brand framing, displays, and section headlines with the clean, neutral precision of **Hanken Grotesk** for analytical interfaces, dashboards, and operational flows.

- **Space Grotesk:** Imparts technical authority and engineered balance. Ideal for multi-sector headline branding, metric callouts, and large-format digital signage.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Delivers crisp clarity across high-density inventory data, fleet coordinates, contractual specifications, and mobile executive summaries.
- **Numeric Handling:** In dense data grids, tables, and live network telemetry, figures utilize tabular lining alignment to guarantee rapid vertical scannability across currency, tonnage, bandwidth, and inventory SKUs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured, fluid enterprise grid engineered for complex operational transparency.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content sits within a max-width container of `1440px`, using `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and generous outer section margins (`3rem`). Complex enterprise views (e.g., global shipping corridors, fibre network distribution) expand to full-bleed dashboards with continuous structural padding.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized `1.5rem` gutters with `2rem` outer page margins, reflowing operational KPI cards into 2-up or 4-up matrices.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Tightened `1rem` gutters with `1.25rem` outer margins. High-density cards adapt to single-column full-width layouts with dedicated horizontal micro-scrolling for sector-level tags and fleet status bars.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing operates strictly on an 8pt architectural grid. Interior card padding uses `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop, contracting to `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, blurred drop shadows, utilizing instead **Tonal Layering** coupled with **Subtle Tinted Contours** and **Micro-Reflective Borders**.

1. **Base Foundation (Level 0):** Canvas surfaces reside on `#F8FAFC`.
2. **Elevated Surface (Level 1 - Cards, Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px structural border of `#E2E8F0`. Depth is conveyed via a subtle dual ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Hover Focus (Level 2):** Elevated cards shift with an expanded lift: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, accompanied by a border state transition to `#0EA5E9` (30% alpha).
4. **Floating Sheets & Dialogs (Level 3):** Modal windows, global fleet filters, and executive action trays utilize `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` over an executive slate backdrop scrim (`#0F172A` at 60% opacity).
5. **Dark Mode Elevation:** In dark command views, depth is achieved purely by shifting surface tonal tiers from `#0F172A` (background) to `#1E293B` (containers) and `#334155` (nested components), accented by 1px borders in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The system adopts a **Soft Geometric (Level 1)** shape language. 

- **Core Elements:** Buttons, inputs, chips, and small controls feature `0.25rem` (`4px`) radius, communicating industrial precision, structural integrity, and modern engineering standards.
- **Containers & Panels:** Cards, multi-metric tiles, and modal structures leverage `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to provide modern ergonomics without sacrificing corporate seriousness.
- **Pill Exception:** Status indicators, real-time connectivity badges, and live logistics trackers may utilize fully rounded pills (`9999px`) to create clear differentiation between static containers and active status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Telecom & Cloud Connectivity):** Solid `#0284C7` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Sharp hover transition to `#0369A1` with an outer focus ring of `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action (Corporate/Executive):** Solid `#0F172A` background with `#FFFFFF` text, shifting to `#1E293B` on hover.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent background with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`. On hover, subtle slate tint `#F1F5F9`.
- **Commerce / Milestone Action:** Amber `#F59E0B` with dark `#0F172A` text for transactional distribution actions and Sweden shipping line triggers.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `0.5rem` corner radius, and 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Sector Division Header:** Cards include an optional top accent edge (2px tall) color-coded by operational vertical: Cyan for Telecom/SaaS, Amber for Logistics/Tyre Distribution/Sweden Route, Slate Navy for Corporate Governance & Infrastructure.
- Padding adheres strictly to `space-lg` (desktop) and `space-md` (mobile).

### Chips & Sector Badges
- Compact indicators using `Hanken Grotesk` `label-sm` with uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.05em`).
- **Active Connectivity (Fibre/Cloud):** Soft cyan background (`#E0F2FE`) with deep cyan text (`#0369A1`).
- **Transit/Logistics (Fleet/Tyres/Sweden Route):** Warm amber tint (`#FEF3C7`) with dark amber text (`#B45309`).
- **Institutional / Infrastructure:** Cool slate tint (`#F1F5F9`) with slate navy text (`#334155`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height of 44px on mobile and 40px on desktop. Surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#CBD5E1`, inner text `#0F172A`. Active state features a crisp `#0284C7` border with a 3px glow (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Square/circular forms bounded by `#94A3B8`. Selected state fills with `#0284C7` and displays an ultra-crisp white check/dot.

### Lists & Data Records
- Alternating subtle rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`), partitioned by hairline borders (`#F1F5F9`).
- Dense mobile list rows leverage a 56px minimum touch target, displaying primary identifiers in `title-md` and secondary telemetry/SKUs in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Enterprise Vertical-Specific Components
- **Transit Metric Tile:** Dedicated mobile-ready container featuring real-time maritime Sweden route status, cargo vessel dispatch, or wholesale tyre inventory metrics with tabular numeric typography and amber status accents.
- **Network Uptime Indicator:** Pulse dot indicator (live `#0EA5E9` pulse) for cloud microservices and telecommunication bandwidth availability across fibre networks.
