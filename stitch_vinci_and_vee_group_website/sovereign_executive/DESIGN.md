## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a commanding, sovereign presence tailored for a multinational holding conglomerate. Designed for C-suite leadership, institutional investors, and global partners, the visual language conveys generational stability, financial precision, and calculated momentum.

The aesthetic fuses modern architectural minimalism with restrained executive luxury. Generous negative space, disciplined geometric alignments, and subtle warm metallic touches reject transient digital gimmicks in favor of permanence. Interactivity is understated, characterized by crisp edge definitions, quiet frosted surfaces, and deliberate mechanical transitions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive hierarchy anchored in deep naval tones and warm architectural metals:

- **Primary (`#0A192F`)**: Midnight Navy serves as the core anchor for dominant structural elements, key action points, deep panels, and high-impact hero fields. An auxiliary tier (`#0D2137`) provides subtle depth in elevated structures.
- **Secondary (`#B37D4E`) & Tertiary (`#6A3E1B`)**: Burnished bronze and deep saddle brown introduce refined metallic warmth without becoming garish. Accent highlights leverage warm amber gold (`#D4A373`) strictly for intentional emphasis and precision borders.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces**: Pure `#FFFFFF` is prohibited across major structural canvases. Backgrounds transition through curated warm ivory and slate-tinted surfaces: Primary Canvas (`#F9FAFC`), Secondary Layering (`#F3F5F9`), and Inset/Structural Surfaces (`#EAEFF5`).
- **Typography & Details (`#0F172A`)**: Dark slate provides crisp, uncompromising legibility across light surfaces without the stark harshness of pitch black.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces high-contrast corporate authority:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides geometric precision and modern architectural stature. Display treatments feature tight tracking and disciplined vertical metrics.
- **Body Text (`Inter`)**: Deployed for dense corporate documentation, annual summaries, and financial reports. Its neutral, systematic structure ensures effortless legibility across extensive copy.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Applied across metadata tags, numerical indices, and interactive actions. Uppercase micro-labels receive wide tracking (+0.06em to +0.08em) to evoke executive financial print standards.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an architectural 12-column fluid grid governed by generous outer margins and disciplined proportional gutters:

- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4-column layout, `1.25rem` canvas margin, `1rem` gutter).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1199px` (8-column layout, `2.5rem` canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutter).
  - Desktop: `≥ 1200px` (12-column layout, `4rem` canvas margin capped at `1440px` content width, `2rem` gutter).
- **Rhythm**: Element padding and section gaps prioritize generous negative space. Major corporate divisions and portfolio showcases demand deliberate breathing room (`space-xl` and higher multiples) to emphasize confidence and structural scale.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via architectural surface layering and refined edge treatment rather than exaggerated drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas surfaces reside on `#F9FAFC`. Elevated content zones sit on `#FFFFFF` or frosted acrylic layers (`rgba(249, 250, 252, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Executive Edge Outlines**: Panels, navigation anchors, and cards utilize subtle hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Active or focused components swap to a refined burnished bronze border (`1px solid rgba(179, 125, 78, 0.45)`).
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: Deep floating cards incorporate highly diffused, navy-tinted ambient occlusion (`0 12px 32px -8px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.03)`).
- **Atmospheric Gradients**: Premium cards and hero zones feature subtle linear surface shifts, spanning from deep midnight navy (`#0A192F`) to obsidian indigo (`#0D2137`) with faint radial bronze reflections (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The system adopts a crisp, tailored shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 

Interactive buttons, inputs, and executive cards employ disciplined `0.25rem` corner radiuses, preserving the sharp, tailored look of architectural drafting. Larger holding modules, hero sections, and executive modal dialogs extend to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), ensuring clean geometric edges that convey institutional durability. Pill shapes, bubbles, and overly softened contours are strictly prohibited.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Deep Midnight Navy (`#0A192F`) fill, crisp `#F9FAFC` text, `0.25rem` corner radius, with a subtle 1px border (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25)`). Transitions to rich burnished amber highlights on hover.
  - *Secondary / Outline*: Transparent fill, 1px solid slate outline (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`), dark slate typography (`#0F172A`). On hover, outlines shift to solid bronze (`#B37D4E`) with an ivory surface tint.
  - *Dimensions*: High-density executive padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`), bold label typography, zero drop-shadow bloat.

- **Cards & Conglomerate Modules**:
  - Encased in warm ivory-white surfaces with a 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
  - Feature structured header dividers, subtle bronze accent lines along top borders for featured ventures, and expansive internal padding (`space-lg`).
  - Dark variants utilize `#0A192F` with inner borders of `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.18)` and ivory secondary text.

- **Navigation**:
  - Translucent frosted header (`rgba(249, 250, 252, 0.88)` with `16px` backdrop blur) coupled with a bottom hairline border (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).
  - Navigation links employ `Plus Jakarta Sans` with muted slate tones, transitioning to deep navy with an anchored bronze underline indicator upon activation.

- **Input Fields & Selectors**:
  - Background sits on `#FFFFFF` with an inset perimeter line (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`).
  - Active focus states introduce an authoritative burnished bronze border (`#B37D4E`) with a delicate box-shadow ring (`0 0 0 1px #B37D4E`).
  - Labels are positioned overhead in uppercase tracked micro-typography (`label-sm`).

- **Lists & Data Grids**:
  - High-density corporate ledger format with horizontal hairline borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
  - Alternating rows may utilize faint `#F3F5F9` striping. Numbers and metrics are aligned right using mono-spaced numeric figures.

- **Chips & Status Tags**:
  - Compact rectangular form factors with sharp `0.25rem` borders.
  - No blinking dots or saturated status rings. Corporate tier tags utilize faint bronze or navy washes (`rgba(179, 125, 78, 0.10)` text `#6A3E1B`, or `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06)` text `#0A192F`) paired with structural border definition.
